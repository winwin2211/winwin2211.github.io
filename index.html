About

I'm a backend and DevOps engineer working on the systems behind on-chain trading at XBIT: a real-time analytics and trading platform and a perpetual futures exchange. I write services in Go and Node.js, run them on Kubernetes across Hetzner, AWS and Azure, and stay on call for both the code and the infrastructure it runs on.

The part of the job I like most is taking a system that is starting to hurt, finding where the time and money actually go, and removing that. That has meant scaling a platform fifty times over without buying fifty times the hardware, cutting a cloud bill by more than half, and keeping trading systems up while releases roll through them.

Before XBIT I built e-commerce and enterprise platforms at Forlife, and GameFi and NFT backends at Lucis Network. I studied computer science at Hanoi University of Science and Technology.
